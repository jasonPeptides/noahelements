My Story我的故事

Noah 源自希伯来古语，寓意安宁、慰藉、庇护与心安。

2025 年，历经一场深刻生命轮回，爱子诺亚降生，至亲父亲安然远行。一生一逝，体悟世间因缘流转与能量循环，这段人生感悟，成就了 Noah Elements 品牌的诞生。

I have studied the Five Elements since I was a child. My father taught me, the way his father taught him. Not as theory, but as a living practice: how to read a constitution, and sense what a person is missing before they can name it.

In 2025, within the same season, I lost my father and welcomed my son. Before he passed, he told me: “What you know isn't for making yourself feel better. It's for giving others something to lean on.”
